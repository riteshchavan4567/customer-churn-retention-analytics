# Business Problem

## Background

The company operates a subscription-based business with a large
customer base. Some customers cancel their subscriptions over time,
resulting in customer churn and potential revenue loss.

## Problem Statement

Management wants to understand customer churn patterns and identify
customers who are at risk of leaving.

## Key Business Questions

1. What is the overall customer churn rate?
2. Which customer segments have the highest churn?
3. Does customer tenure affect churn?
4. Does subscription or contract type affect churn?
5. Does customer engagement affect churn?
6. Does payment behavior affect churn?
7. Does customer support experience affect churn?
8. Which customers are high-value customers?
9. How much revenue is associated with churned customers?
10. How much revenue is potentially at risk?
11. Which customers should be prioritized for retention?
12. What retention strategies can the company consider?

## Project Objective

Analyze customer behavior, churn patterns, and revenue contribution to
identify high-risk and high-value customers, quantify revenue at risk,
and provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention.

## Expected Business Outcome

The analysis will help management understand the major patterns
associated with customer churn and prioritize retention efforts toward
customers and segments where intervention may have the greatest
business impact.
