# Customer Churn & Retention Analytics

## Project Overview

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding customer
churn, identifying high-value customers at risk, analyzing revenue at
risk, and developing data-driven customer retention strategies.

## Business Problem

The company wants to understand:

- Why customers are leaving
- Which customers are at higher risk of churn
- Which customer segments are most valuable
- How much revenue is at risk
- What actions can improve customer retention

## Project Objectives

- Analyze customer behavior
- Calculate churn and retention metrics
- Identify churn patterns
- Analyze customer value
- Identify revenue at risk
- Segment customers based on value and risk
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Provide actionable business recommendations
